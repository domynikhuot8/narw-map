North Atlantic Right Whale Monitoring

Data presented is based on an ongoing study of the North Atlantic right whale, looking at ways to detect them in zones of high sea traffic in an attempt to create a warning system to protect this endangered species.

How to use this map:

  • Use the legend on the right to navigate the layers
  • Data is grouped by time period; each period has its own RCM imagery group just below it
  • Check and uncheck groups or layers to display the data you want
  • Hover over or click a feature to see its details; move into the popup to keep it open
  • Use the zoom controls or scroll wheel to navigate

Periods: the most recent period is shown when the map opens. Tick an earlier period in the legend to compare.
