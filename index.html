North Atlantic Right Whale Monitoring

Data presented is based on an ongoing study of the North Atlantic right whale, looking at ways to detect them in zones of high sea traffic in an attempt to create a warning system to protect this endangered species.

How to use this map:

  • Use the legend on the right to navigate different layers and data sets
  • Check and uncheck layers to display desired data
  • Click on individual features to see detailed information
  • Use the zoom controls or scroll wheel to navigate

Data Retention: Detections and platform tracks are displayed for approximately one week to reflect the dynamic movements of whales and ongoing survey operations.
